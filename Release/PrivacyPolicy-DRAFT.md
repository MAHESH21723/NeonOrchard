# Neon Orchard privacy policy

Draft for publisher review. Replace the contact fields below before publishing this policy at a public URL.

Effective date: 7 October 2026

Neon Orchard is an offline Snake game. It does not require an account and has no advertising, in-app purchases, social features, or gameplay analytics service.

## Data on your device

The game stores your high score, fruit total, level progress, star ratings, selected color, and audio/control preferences locally on your device. These are used to restore your progress and preferences. The game does not transmit this information to the developer or other parties.

## Collection and sharing

This version does not collect or share personal information through the game. Unity analytics, engine diagnostics and crash reporting are disabled in the project. There are no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs integrated into the gameplay.

## Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you clear the app's storage or remove the app. Device or operating-system backup behavior is controlled by your device settings. There is no game account or developer-held gameplay record to delete.

## Device features

Optional vibration is used only for local gameplay feedback. The game does not request camera, microphone, contact, or location access.

## Support

If you contact the developer outside the game, the developer receives the information you choose to send and uses it to answer your request.
