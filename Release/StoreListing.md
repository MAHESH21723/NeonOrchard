# Neon Orchard: Snake

Short description:

A fresh take on Snake. Explore 24 levels, chase scores and find your flow offline.

Full description:

Small turns. Big possibilities.

Neon Orchard gives the classic Snake game a fresh, calm look. Guide your snake through a colorful orchard, collect coral fruit and enjoy a little moment of play.

GROW THROUGH THE JOURNEY
Explore 24 stages across four chapters. Harvest each level's fruit target, navigate new obstacles and earn stars by finishing efficiently.

FIND YOUR FLOW
Play Endless mode to chase your personal best. The pace gradually rises as your snake grows.

MAKE IT YOURS
Collect fruit to unlock Lagoon, Blossom and Golden colors. All unlocks are earned through play. Set music, sound effects, vibration and direction buttons to suit you.

SIMPLE CONTROLS
Swipe anywhere to turn, or use the large on-screen direction buttons. Pause whenever you need a break.

PLAY OFFLINE
No ads, no in-app purchases and no account required. Your progress is saved on your device.

Features:
- 24 stages in four chapters
- Endless score mode
- Four snake colors
- Original mellow music and sound effects
- Swipe controls and optional direction buttons
- Local scores, stars and saved progress
